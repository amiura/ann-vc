04The Harness
The scaffolding that turns a capable model into reliable work — it bundles the connections, memory, skills, permissions, a verification loop & the interface into one environment.

01 DATA

The systems of record and a defined, trustworthy version of the business the agent acts on — not a raw data lake.

What it is

Connected sources (Slack, Notion, Salesforce, Gong, Snowflake, GitHub) + a curated metric layer — definitions, date grains, calc logic all locked — plus proprietary first-party data.

Why it matters: the bottleneck is data legibility, not the model. Undefined metrics and silos are what break agentic inquiry.

Ramp: auto-connects ~16 systems; "Ramp Research" as one canonical data house · Roo: 350-metric semantic layer; first-party hospital data

02 CONTEXT

What the agent knows about you and your world, recorded & persisted so it never starts cold.

What we record

Who you are, who you work with, your active projects, your communication style, where you sit in the org — mined from connected tools, written to memory, injected into every session, refreshed daily.

Why it matters: effectiveness = context quality. It's portable and gets more valuable as the models improve.

Ramp Glass: memory is the "single magic feature," 24-hr refresh · Roo: a personal brain + a company brain

03 SKILLS

Reusable, packaged know-how — one person's figured-out workflow, encoded in plain language so anyone can run it. Versioned & reviewed like code.

Examples

"Analyze a Gong call → draft a battlecard," a Zendesk investigation flow, growth-accounting playbooks (human owns the method, model executes). Skills → workflows → agents.

Why it matters: one person's breakthrough becomes everyone's baseline — the compounding flywheel, and the bridge for non-engineers.

Ramp Dojo: 350+ skills, Git-backed; "Sensei" recommends them · Roo playbooks · Harvey
