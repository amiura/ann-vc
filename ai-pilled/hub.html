The AI-Pilled Enterprise

What can AI see, do, extend, and change?

Most "AI transformation" conversations measure the wrong thing; they count seats, pilots, and trainings and conclude the company is further along than it is. These materials measure something harder to fake: how much of your organization's real work AI can now see, do, extend, and change.

They come from a maturity model built on field visits to the most AI-native companies operating today — organizations whose operating model has changed because AI can read the work, act on it, and improve it without routing through human bureaucracy. That is the bar that separates the companies that compound from the ones that plateau.
